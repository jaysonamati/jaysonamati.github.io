## A note on an application of live decoding

Assuming we have a good conceptualization and operationalization of [live discernment](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/LqsctjJbk5HHPD4Jm/the-math-and-the-territory-1), what can we do with it?

A good way to think about live discernment is by the word or process of decoding. We are taking a formal representation of an idea (which can be thought of as a result of an encoding process) and decoding it to the “larger story of being” that led to its creation. Once we have embedded the formalization in this larger context, we can begin to connect it to other contexts we initially didn’t care about, thereby expanding the formalization's circle of care. We can also apply this not only to formal artefacts but also to digital representations of the self. Finding a good “formal” representation of “what we care about” affords us computational techniques for expanding the sphere of care. Imagine “what we care about” to be a sphere of a certain radius; we could have a practice, an institution, a protocol, a computation, a program, or an algorithm to expand the sphere’s radius so that we care about more things. Or be nudged to care about things we hadn’t considered before.

Notice we don’t mean caring more in the sense of reading a book, attending a seminar, or enrolling in a course, but a meaningful shift in being, in the sense of having, as some would put it, a spiritual experience.

# Influences: Category Theory

This notion of expanding the network/circle of care of an arbitrary entity was partly inspired by constructions found in a branch of mathematics (meta-math) called [category theory](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/category+theory). Category theory is said to be the language of composition; I like to think of it as the language of interaction. It is also easy to see that interaction and composition are related concepts. You can’t have interaction without some form of composition.

Suppose you are given a [Vector Space](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/vector+space) and a [Set](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/set). How would you go about relating them? How do you allow an interaction between them? If you are familiar with Category Theory, then it is obvious where this is going. We’re going to create a [category](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/category) of Vectors and another category of Sets and related structures from one category to another by constructing a [functor](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/functor) between them. We can also have multiple ways of relating vectors and sets, and relate the different ways of relating vectors and sets, and so on. This can go on forever in the right category. More compelling and successful examples of cross-field interactions in mathematics can be found in [algebraic topology](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/algebraic+topology).

By putting a mathematical notion in this “larger” space, we, in a sense, increase its interaction potential. This is an example of what we refer to as expanding its sphere/network of care; it can affect more diverse areas and be affected by them.
